❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CollabSpace free to use? +
Yes! CollabSpace is completely free to sign up and start using. Our core real-time messaging features are available for everyone at no cost.
How does real-time messaging work? +
We use Socket.io — a powerful WebSocket library — to deliver messages in real-time with sub-100ms latency. Your messages appear instantly on the recipient's screen.
Is my data secure? +
Absolutely. CollabSpace uses secure authentication and your conversations are stored safely in our database. We never share or sell your data to third parties.
Can I use it on mobile? +
Yes! CollabSpace is fully responsive and works beautifully on mobile browsers. We're also working on dedicated mobile apps coming soon!
How many users can chat simultaneously? +
CollabSpace supports thousands of concurrent users. Our Socket.io backend is designed for high concurrency with efficient room-based messaging architecture.
